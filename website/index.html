Visual Identity

Narratix Modern Dark Design System

A purposeful, restrained visual framework built for high visual endurance. Prioritizes data readability, cinematic hierarchy, and tactile clarity over generic SaaS decoration.

1. Deep Space Canvas

The fundamental backdrop is calibrated at #0B0B0C, not pitch black or washed-out navy. This deep matte tone eliminates eye strain during multi-hour script analysis.

2. Electric Violet Identity

Primary brand accents use Electric Violet (#7C3AED) with optical balance against emerald (#34D399) and amber (#FBBF24) for quantitative telemetry.

3. Restrained Luminescence

Zero neon overload or gratuitous glassmorphism. Borders remain at 8%–12% opacity with precise 1px micro-dividers that guide visual navigation naturally.
